We are so glad you’re here. After Houston in 2024, the Austin family gathers again in 2026 — this time in Fresno, California. This page has everything you need: how to register, the weekend schedule, where to stay, and a look back at the reunions that built us.

No. 02The Weekend

Three days. One family.

Here’s the plan for the weekend in Fresno.

09
Fri · Oct 9 · Evening

Family Meet & Greet

Join us Friday evening to kick off the weekend. We’re finalizing details and have a few exciting options to be announced as we get closer to the event.

The reunion begins ✦
10
Sat · Oct 10 · All Day

Family Fun Day at Blackbeard’s

An afternoon of food, games and activities for all ages at Blackbeard’s Family Entertainment Center. Catered lunch included with registration; kids 12 & under get a ride wristband (extras for 13+ are $26).

  • Family group photos
  • Games for kids, teens & adults
  • Music and dancing
  • Vote: the 2028 reunion city
Lunch & kids’ wristband included ✦
11
Sun · Oct 11 · Morning

Madera Cemetery Visit

We close the weekend at Madera Cemetery, paying tribute to our ancestors and placing flowers on grave sites — then a final excursion before heading home. Charter bus included, or drive yourself.

Honoring those who came before ✦
