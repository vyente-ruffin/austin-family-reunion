The Weekend

Three days in Fresno — here's the plan.

Friday, October 9 · Evening

Family Meet & Greet

Join us Friday evening for our Family Meet & Greet! We're finalizing details and have a few exciting options to be announced as we get closer to the event.

Saturday, October 10 · All Day

Family Fun Day at Blackbeard's

Start your morning with breakfast at your hotel and take some time to relax or explore Fresno. At Blackbeard's Family Entertainment Center, the festivities begin in the afternoon — enjoy food, games, and activities for all ages. Lunch will be catered and is included in the cost of registration. Children 12 and under will also receive a wristband for access to the rides, included with their registration. Additional wristbands for those 13+ can be purchased for $26.

  • Family group photos
  • Games for kids, teens, and adults
  • Music and dancing
  • A vote for the 2028 reunion location
Sunday, October 11 · Morning

Madera Cemetery Visit & Excursion

We'll close out the weekend with a visit to the Madera Cemetery to pay tribute to our ancestors and place flowers on grave sites. Transportation is provided via charter bus (included with your registration), or you're welcome to drive yourself — just let us know your preference on the registration form. After the cemetery visit will be a final excursion before returning to Fresno to depart.
