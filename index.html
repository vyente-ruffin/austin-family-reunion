The Weekend

Three days in Fresno — here's the plan.

Friday, October 9 · Evening

Family Meet & Greet

Join us Friday evening for our Family Meet & Greet. We're finalizing the details and have a few exciting options to be announced as we get closer to the event.

Saturday, October 10 · All Day

Family Fun Day at Blackbeard's

Start your morning with breakfast at your hotel, then relax or explore Fresno. In the afternoon, the festivities begin at Blackbeard's Family Entertainment Center — food, games, and activities for all ages. Lunch is catered and included. Children 12 and under receive a ride wristband at no extra cost; additional wristbands for ages 13+ may be purchased for $40.

  • Family group photos
  • Games for kids, teens, and adults
  • Music and dancing
  • A vote for the 2028 reunion location
Sunday, October 11 · Morning

Madera Cemetery Tribute

We close the weekend with a visit to the Madera Cemetery to honor our ancestors and place flowers on grave sites. Transportation is provided by charter bus and is included in your registration — whether or not you join the Sunday excursion. A final excursion follows before we return to Fresno to depart.
